Mushishi

蟲師

Quiet stories at the edge of the natural world.

Slow down
A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

The mushishi Ginko travels from place to place, resolving strange phenomena that arise between people and the primordial life forms called Mushi. Each episode often begins with one village or one person’s trouble.

Why watch

It avoids simple good-or-evil judgments and lets nature, illness and human choice weave together. Good for a quiet watch of standalone stories.

For you if…

For folk atmosphere, natural imagery and independent episodic storytelling.

Before you start

The pace is gentle; some stories involve illness, loss and unease.
