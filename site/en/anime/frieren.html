A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

The heroes have already returned home when Frieren’s story begins. For an elf, a decade slips by almost unnoticed. For her human companions, those same years meant a great deal.

Why watch

The small things carry the story: a remembered habit, an old promise, a spell someone once loved. The adventure gives those memories room to breathe.

For you if…

For evenings when you want magic, landscapes and a little quiet reflection.

Before you start

Loss is part of the story. Give it time if you usually prefer fast-paced action.
