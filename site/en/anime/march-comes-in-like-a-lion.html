March Comes in Like a Lion

3月のライオン

A young player finds a place to belong.

Slow down
A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

Professional shogi player Rei Kiriyama lives alone and carries the pressure of matches and growing up. After meeting the Kawamoto sisters, his daily life begins to gain warmth.

Why watch

Shogi is only part of the story. More important is how the character faces loneliness, family and self-worth. It suits viewers who are glad to let emotions unfold slowly.

For you if…

For growth stories, gentle everyday life and interior character writing.

Before you start

It touches on loneliness, family relations and school bullying, some of it heavy.
