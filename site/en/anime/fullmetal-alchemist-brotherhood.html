A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

The Elric brothers pay a heavy price for an alchemical experiment. To recover what they lost, they join the military and follow clues to the Philosopher’s Stone.

Why watch

It pairs adventure and battle with a fully built world. The story keeps asking about cost, responsibility and the promises people keep to one another.

For you if…

If you like a clear goal, a brotherly bond and a long, grand fantasy world.

Before you start

It touches on war, death, human experimentation and political conflict, so the emotional intensity runs high.
