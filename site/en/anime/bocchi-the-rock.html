A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

Hitori Gotoh can play guitar but struggles to talk to people. After joining Kessoku Band, she has to slowly get past her nervousness in rehearsals, gigs and friendships.

Why watch

It turns social anxiety into exaggerated, cute visual gags while keeping the character’s real fear and effort intact. A good pick for an evening that needs light encouragement.

For you if…

For music, school everyday life and an introvert coming-of-age.

Before you start

The comedy moves fast, and the protagonist’s anxiety is played up for effect.
