Kaguya-sama: Love Is War

Unwind
A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

Student council president Miyuki Shirogane and vice president Kaguya Shinomiya like each other but refuse to admit it first. Their daily standoffs grow into a campus story around a whole group of classmates.

Why watch

It turns romantic hesitation into an exaggerated tactical duel. The pace is light, and the character interactions gain depth as the episodes go on.

For you if…

For school comedy, romantic push-and-pull and an ensemble cast.

Before you start

The humour relies heavily on dialogue, over-the-top narration and Japanese school situations.
