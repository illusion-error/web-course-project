Chihayafuru

ちはやふる

A card game becomes a shared dream.

Find courage · Feel the energy
A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

Chihaya Ayase discovers competitive karuta through a friend and starts a club in high school. Memory, speed and team goals give the traditional game a tense competitive edge.

Why watch

It makes unfamiliar karuta rules easy to follow, yet goes beyond winning and losing. The characters’ different ideas of dreams, friendship and competition keep the story moving.

For you if…

For competition, club growth and a youth ensemble.

Before you start

It draws on classical Japanese poetry and the Hyakunin Isshu, so the start takes some getting used to.
