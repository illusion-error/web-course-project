A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

The powerful esper Shigeo “Mob” Kageyama works with the psychic Reigen Arataka on odd spirit cases, while trying to express himself and make friends.

Why watch

The action bursts with energy, but the core stays growth and self-acceptance. It asks not who is strongest, but how a person uses their power.

For you if…

For supernatural action, an odd mentor pairing and youth growth.

Before you start

It includes spirit exorcisms, eerie imagery and intense action scenes.
