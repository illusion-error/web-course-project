Natsume’s Book of Friends

Small encounters between people and spirits.

A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

Natsume can see spirits. A book left by his grandmother draws him into their lives, one encounter and one name at a time.

Why watch

Each meeting leaves space for a spirit’s side of the story. The quieter moments often linger longest.

For you if…

For viewers drawn to folklore, kindness and stories about belonging.

Before you start

Some encounters are lonely or unsettling. Start with the first TV season.
