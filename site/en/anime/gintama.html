A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

In an Edo visited by aliens, Gintoki Sakata and his odd-job crew take on ordinary requests that always seem to spiral out of control.

Why watch

It switches between over-the-top comedy and serious arcs. Treat it first as a collection of character jokes and daily gags, then decide whether to follow the longer story lines.

For you if…

If you enjoy dense jokes, a large cast and sharp changes in tone.

Before you start

Much of the humour leans on Japanese pop culture and period references, and the long run also includes serious battle arcs.
