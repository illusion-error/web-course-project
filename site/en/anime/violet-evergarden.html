STORY 19 / Coming of age

Violet Evergarden

ヴァイオレット・エヴァーガーデン

Letters help a young woman learn what feelings mean.

Slow down
A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

After the war, Violet, who has lost her place to belong, becomes a ghostwriter of letters. She enters different clients’ lives and tries to understand a phrase she once heard.

Why watch

Each letter opens an independent emotional entry into a relationship. Good for finely drawn visuals and stories about communication and loss.

For you if…

For letters, emotional growth and detailed visual craft.

Before you start

It touches on war trauma, death and parting, with some high emotional concentration.
