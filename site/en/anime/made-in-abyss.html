Made in Abyss

メイドインアビス

A vast abyss calls explorers ever deeper.

Slow down
A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

Riko lives in a town built around a vast abyss. She dreams of becoming a Cave Raider and sets out downward with the robot boy she meets.

Why watch

The worldbuilding and sense of exploration are strong; each layer down changes the rules and the risk. It suits fans of unknown maps, adventure settings and the thrill of discovery.

For you if…

For fantasy exploration, mysterious ruins and world-building.

Before you start

It looks cute but includes bodily harm, horror and high-intensity emotional content.
