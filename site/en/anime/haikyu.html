A STORY FOR TONIGHT

The story

Hinata joins his high school volleyball club and finds that a former rival is now his teammate. On the court, neither can win a point alone.

Why watch

Follow the ball for one rally. A receive gives the setter a chance, and the set gives someone else a shot. That chain makes the teamwork easy to feel.

For you if…

For viewers who enjoy competition, practice and the thrill of a close point.

Before you start

You can start without knowing the rules. The first season introduces the players and their roles.
